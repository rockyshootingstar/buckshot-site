Thanks for testing! For Mac (macOS 14 or later).

Download for Mac

Always the newest version. Once it's installed, Buckshot updates itself.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded Buckshot.dmg and drag Buckshot onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open Buckshot from Applications. The first time, your Mac may say it can't be opened (Buckshot isn't sold through Apple). That's expected:
    • Click Done (not Move to Trash).
    • Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Buckshot. Enter your Mac password if asked.
    • Click Open once more. You only do this once.
  3. Click Sign in with Google. If Google says it hasn't verified Buckshot yet, click Continue. When Google shows two boxes, tick both, then Continue.
  4. Click OK for sending iMessages and Allow for notifications. A short tour shows you the rest.

Found a bug or have an idea? Click Report Issue / Feedback at the top of Buckshot.
