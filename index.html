Buckshot

Send files to your people, one individual message each.

Buckshot is a small Mac app for musicians and creators who send the same files (beats, loops, demos) to lots of collaborators. Pick a list, write a message, drop in your files, and Buckshot sends each person their own individual message by iMessage or email. No group chats, no reply-all.

What it does

  • Keeps your collaborators in lists, like “pain loops” or “trap beats”.
  • Sends one personal message per person, with {name} filled in with their first name.
  • Sends by iMessage (through the Messages app on your Mac), by email, or both. Everyone gets one message.
  • Warns you if you've already sent a file to someone.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to send email from Gmail, Buckshot asks Google for permission to send email on your behalf (the gmail.send permission), plus your name and email address to show which account you're sending from. For files too big to attach (over 25 MB), it can put them in a “Buckshot” folder in your own Google Drive and link them in the email, like Gmail does; it can only touch files it created. Buckshot cannot read, search or delete your email. It only sends the messages you write, to the people you choose, when you press Send.

Everything else (your contacts, lists, drafts and history) stays on your Mac. Buckshot has no servers and no accounts of its own.
