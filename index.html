How it works

One send-out. Every person gets it on their own.

1

Pick a list

Keep your collaborators in lists, like “pain loops” or “trap beats”.

2

Drop in your files

Beats, loops, demos. Write one message; {name} becomes each person's first name. If anyone on the list already has a file, Buckshot warns you before it sends.

3

Send

Everyone gets their own message from your number or email. Your Mac does all the sending, so the whole list is done in moments, not an hour of copy-paste.

What it does

Made for sending files, not for group chats.

No more leaked contacts

Every email goes out on its own, to one person. There's no BCC to forget, so nobody ever sees who else is on your list.

Never send the same file twice

Buckshot remembers every file you've sent to every person. Before anything goes out, it tells you who already has it, so nobody gets the same file twice and you never look sloppy.

See who opened it

Turn on open tracking and Buckshot shows you who opened your email and when, so you know exactly who to follow up with.

Texts, email or both

Texts go out through the Messages app on your Mac; email through Gmail or your own provider. Everyone gets one message.

Schedule for later

Set a send-out for the perfect moment and Buckshot sends it right on time, even with the app closed.

Privacy & signing in with Google

If you choose to send email from Gmail, Buckshot asks Google for permission to send email on your behalf (the gmail.send permission), plus your name and email address to show which account you're sending from. For files too big to attach (over 25 MB), it can put them in a “Buckshot” folder in your own Google Drive and link them in the email, like Gmail does; it can only touch files it created. Buckshot cannot read, search or delete your email. It only sends the messages you write, to the people you choose, when you press Send.

Your Google sign-in is also your Buckshot account: Buckshot's server keeps your email address and which plan you're on, nothing else. Everything else (your contacts, lists, drafts and history) stays on your Mac.

Before you download

If something goes wrong or you have an idea, click Report Issue / Feedback at the top of Buckshot. It goes straight to us and helps us fix it fast.
